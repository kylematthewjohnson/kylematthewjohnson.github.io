DESCRIPTION:
Reckoning presents a series of imagined interactions between different versions of Kyrin throughout his dance career and beyond. Each dancer represents Kyrin at an important crossroads in life. This was the first major piece of choreography Kyrin created after returning to dance after school and starting a new career as a data scientist.
